# 🧠 Brain Note - Continuous Writing Challenge App

A unique, mindfulness-based note-taking application designed to encourage **continuous, uninterrupted writing** and deep focus. If you stop typing, your written content disappears—a powerful mechanism to keep your mind engaged and flowing.

---

## 🎯 Overview

**Brain Note** is a productivity tool that combines the benefits of free-writing with the psychological principle of momentum. The app requires you to keep typing continuously. Once you pause for a set duration, all your notes are cleared, encouraging you to maintain your stream of consciousness and stay in a state of flow.

## 🚀 How to Use Brain Note

### **Step 1: Open the Application**
Simply open `index.html` in your web browser to launch Brain Note.

### **Step 2: Set Your Timer**
- Look for the **"Reset in (seconds):"** input field at the top
- Enter the desired timeout duration (e.g., 3, 5, or 10 seconds)
- This is how long you can pause before your text is cleared

### **Step 3: Start Writing**
- Click the **"Start"** button to begin
- A timer will appear showing the countdown
- Start typing in the text area

### **Step 4: Keep Typing!**
- ✅ **Keep typing continuously** - your timer resets with each keystroke
- ✅ Your timer counts down in real-time
- ⏸️ **Stop typing** - if you pause for the duration you set, your text will be cleared

### **Step 5: Save Your Work (Optional)**
- Click the **"Copy"** button to copy your text to clipboard
- Paste it elsewhere to save your work
- Click **"Stop"** to stop the timer without losing your text

## 🎮 Usage Tips & Strategies

### **For Beginners:**
- Start with a **longer timer** (10-15 seconds) to get comfortable
- Gradually reduce the time as you build momentum
- Set a goal: "Write for 5 minutes without stopping"

### **For Maximum Productivity:**
- Use **3-5 seconds** for intense, focused bursts
- Perfect for brainstorming, quick thoughts, or stream-of-consciousness writing
- Combine with other techniques: pomodoro timers, scheduled breaks

### **For Journaling & Reflection:**
- Use a **longer timer** (30+ seconds) for deeper introspection
- Great for morning pages or evening reflection
- Combine with meditation for enhanced mindfulness

### **Writing Exercises:**
- **Free Writing:** Let your thoughts flow naturally
- **Prompt-based:** Use writing prompts to guide your focus
- **Timed Challenges:** Compete with yourself to write more in less time
